## Assignment arrangements

- Detailed assessment instructions are provided on D2L.
- Each assignment includes coding and demo components. Attend the tutorial in which you are enrolled.
- Group work is allowed only when the individual assignment description permits it. Partners must register for and attend the same tutorial.
- Late assignments receive a penalty for each day late; submissions more than two days late are not accepted. The outline does not state a numerical penalty rate.
- For extenuating circumstances, contact your TA and Head TA about alternative arrangements.

## Academic integrity and course materials

- Submitted work must be your own. Do not look at, submit, or exchange other students' solutions.
- Disclose the source and extent of help received from people or AI other than a TA or instructor.
- Small amounts of external code (1–3 lines) may be used with a citation in a comment. The outline says a significant borrowed portion, such as more than 10%, receives no assignment credit even if cited.
- No aids are allowed in exams.
- The outline states that course materials are instructor intellectual property and prohibits unauthorized sharing, distribution, or posting. Consult the complete policy in the PDF and D2L.
